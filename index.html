About the Firm

A premier Ahmedabad firm delivering strategy under one roof

Established in 2010, Keval Ponkiya & Associates is a premier Ahmedabad-based Chartered Accountancy firm delivering multi-disciplinary financial, tax, corporate-law and management-consulting services under one roof.

Driven by a senior team comprising Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, Cost Accountants, Advocates and Management Consultants, the firm provides end-to-end strategic solutions across diverse business domains.

What We Do

Full-Spectrum Services & Advisory

From GST & indirect-tax litigation to audit, taxation, corporate law and business setup — comprehensive solutions delivered under one roof.

GST Returns & Compliance

Seamless monthly/quarterly preparation, verification and filing of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9 (Annual Return) and GSTR-9C (Reconciliation Statement).

ITC Optimization & Reconciliation

In-depth ITC optimization, GSTR-2B vs. 3B matching, electronic ledger analysis, vendor error tracking and liability reconciliation.

Impact & Structural Analysis

Strategic advisory on business structuring, supply-chain evaluation, SEZ/EOU units and sector-specific implications across Real Estate, IT/ITeS, Manufacturing & Exports.

Registration & Modifications

End-to-end GST registration, core/non-core amendments, voluntary cancellation and filing for revocation of cancelled GSTINs.

Departmental Representation

Professional representation before GST Authorities, Scrutiny Officers, Assessment Officers and Special Auditors.

Notices & SCN Replies

Robust, legally sound responses to GST notices — ASMT-10, DRC-01, Show Cause Notices and departmental audit queries.

Appeals & Dispute Resolution

End-to-end appeals — Grounds of Appeal, Statement of Facts and Condonation of Delay — up to Appellate Authorities and Tax Tribunals.

Refunds & Export Incentives

Assistance claiming inverted-duty-structure refunds, zero-rated export refunds and Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) scheme benefits.

Annual ROC Compliances

Navigating India's regulatory landscape is crucial for every business. Our Annual ROC Compliance Package is a comprehensive solution designed to keep your company fully compliant and thriving within the framework of guidelines and regulations.

Secretarial Services

Company Secretaries play a crucial role in the efficient management of daily operations — drafting correspondence, generating reports and preparing documents including meeting minutes, working closely with the board of directors.

Company Valuation Services

Keval Ponkiya & Associates specializes in Company Valuation Services, providing reliable and comprehensive valuations for businesses across various industries — including manufacturing, energy, transportation and more.

Broad Practice Areas

Statutory & Tax Audit

Statutory Audits under the Companies Act, Tax Audits under the Income Tax Act and Limited Reviews.

Risk & Management Advisory

Internal Audits, Management Assurance, Internal Financial Control (IFC) reporting, SOP development and manuals.

Specialized Audits

Audit of Banks, Charitable Trusts, Educational Institutions, NGOs and Co-operative Societies.

Financial Verification

Physical stock verification & valuation, fixed-asset reconciliation, revenue assurance, due diligence and IFRS implementation.

Assessment & Disputes

Verification of assessment orders, filing appeals and representation before CIT(A), ITAT and the Settlement Commission.

International Tax & DTAA

Transfer-pricing documentation, Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement advisory and cross-border structuring.

FEMA & Remittances

Complete FEMA compliance, RBI approvals/permissions and inward/outward remittance certifications.

Tax Planning

Direct-tax planning for Residents, NRIs, Foreign Nationals and Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).

Company Formation

Incorporation of listed & unlisted companies, foreign companies, LLPs and Section 8 entities.

Statutory Representation

Representation before ROC, NCLT, SEBI, Stock Exchanges (BSE/NSE) and the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).

Corporate Restructuring

Capital reorganization, mergers, amalgamations, takeovers, liquidations and secretarial audits.

Statutory Compliances

Labour-law compliances and liaisoning with various Government and Semi-Government authorities.

Business Structuring

Advice on suitable legal entities — Proprietorship, LLP, Company or Trust — and drafting of deed clauses.

Project Finance & Syndication

Preparation of CMA data, financial presentations and syndication of debt from Banks, VCs and NBFCs.

Incentives & Subsidies

Optimizing government incentive schemes, subsidies and tax benefits — e.g. 80IA, 80JJA and SEZ/SIR policies.

Cross-Border Business

Cross-border entity setup, FII/FDI registrations and foreign-entity establishment in India and abroad.
